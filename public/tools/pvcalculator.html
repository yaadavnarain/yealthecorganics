How this is sized: panels and battery cover a normal worst-month (June) day in Flacq at about 4.0 kWh per kWp. The generator, set to auto-start near 20 to 30% battery and stop near 80 to 85%, covers longer bad weather so you do not pay for a huge idle battery.
